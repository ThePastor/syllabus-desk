# Syllabus Desk

### ▶︎ Open the app: **https://thepastor.github.io/syllabus-desk/**

*This repository is the source code. The link above is the actual working site.*

---

Turn your course outlines into one calendar for the whole term.

Add each course once — paste the outline text, or drop in the PDF or Word file your instructor
posted. Claude reads it and pulls out the exams, quizzes, reports and deadlines, and **you confirm
every date before anything is saved.** Anything the outline left blank is flagged rather than
guessed.

What you get back:

- **This week and next**, day by day, with everything you owe
- A **full-term calendar** you can filter by course
- **Course by course** — grade weightings, class schedule, and the rule most likely to cost you marks
- **Weekly study blocks**, plus extra prep 5, 3 and 1 days before every exam
- **Export** to Google Calendar (`.csv`) or Apple Calendar (`.ics`), so reminders reach your phone

Your outlines are read in your browser and the file is never uploaded. Your schedule is saved in
your own browser's storage — not on a server, and not visible to anyone else. The one exception,
stated plainly: when you press **Read this outline**, the extracted text of that outline is sent to
Anthropic's Claude to be turned into dates.

It uses an AI model to read outlines, so **it will sometimes get a date wrong.** It is a planning
aid, not a source of truth — your course page and your instructor are.
